Direction

Blush ground, blush action

The terrazzo floor becomes the interface. Warm blush paper, a deeper blush for anything the client taps, brass hairlines — the boutique read in daylight rather than at night.

Typography

LA SIRÈNE

Bodoni Moda — headings and the wordmark, tracked wide to match the storefront signage. Archivo Light — body, labels and all uppercase micro-type.

What changed

Light ground, dark type. A deeper blush carries every filled surface — buttons, the logo circle, the active segment, the ready badge — while lapis survives only as type: links, the active tab label, the confirmed state. Brass darkens to hold contrast on a pale surface. The renderings supply the only large areas of colour, framed in the arch.
